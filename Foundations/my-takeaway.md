# My Takeaway — Foundations: Roles and Concepts

## What clicked
The Data Discovery process finally made sense after finishing this module — how all
these services have a purpose and a need, not just a concept of quantity over quality.
Good questioning and planning before implementing is the core of engineering here,
which reminded me of the Requirements Analysis step in the software development lifecycle.

## What surprised me
At first glance, the list of 20+ services was overwhelming — but it's actually a set
of objects orbiting a center of gravity (S3). Every service exists to serve a purpose
around it.

## What I want to dig deeper into
Step Functions vs. Lambda for pipeline orchestration is something I want to understand
better — both appeared in the monitoring section as workflow tools, but when do you
choose one over the other? That decision probably has a big impact on how a pipeline
is structured and debugged.

## How this connects to what I already know
In DataNexus, we made early architectural decisions without a formal discovery process
— and some of the integration issues we hit later were a direct result of that. Going
through the Data Discovery steps here made me realize that the five foundational
questions aren't theoretical, they're the exact things we had to reverse-engineer
mid-project to fix things.
